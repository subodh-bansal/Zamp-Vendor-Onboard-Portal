Before You Start

Welcome to our vendor onboarding portal. Please review this guide before submitting your application.

How Onboarding Works

  1. Fill Details: Complete your company and banking information.
  2. Upload Documents: Provide your GST Certificate, PAN Card, and Cancelled Cheque (PDF, JPG, PNG up to 5 MB).
  3. Automated Review: Our system validates your tax IDs and documentation in minutes.
  4. Outcome: Receive instant approval or clear, actionable instructions if any corrections are needed.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Ensure your legal business name matches perfectly across your tax certificates, PAN card, and bank account holder name to avoid review delays.
